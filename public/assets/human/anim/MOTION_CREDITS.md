# Motion capture credits

The clips in `clips.bin` / `clips.json` were built by `tools/assets/motion/cmu.mjs` from trials of the
**CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database** (http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu). The database was created with funding
from NSF EIA-0196217 and may be used for any purpose; this credit is the only condition.
