# CHARACTERS — SECRET BIBLE (never quote to the owner)

Every named person who matters, written as a person: what they want, what they fear, where they contradict
themselves, how they talk, what they hide, what they think of each other, how their arc moves, and what
breaks them. Content ids stay neutral (`npc_<place>_<nn>`). Looks are briefs for `randomAppearance`/`dressFor`
(ancestry preset, frame, age, one or two distinctive features, outfit role, chrome).

Voice rule: nobody speaks the theme as a slogan. Everyone has a verbal habit, a thing they never say, and a
subject that makes them talk too much. Dialogue is never expository for its own sake: people say what they
would actually say to this person, now.

### comp_tamsin — Tamsin Roe (24, Cistern 9)

  "please". Talks too much about the surface; never about the clinic months of her childhood.
- **Secret:** she is **unforecast** — MOTHER hid her birth (born sickly, "unfit for relocation") so the city
  never counted her. She half-knows: her name isn't on any ration list before age six.

  "stolen" lives), Ash (suspicious of vows), Nine ("it's a fridge with legs"), Odile (bores her), Ilya

  dies at the Cistern door if the player sides with force and she stands in it.
- **Red line:** siding with relocating the Cistern by force. **Breaks:** being told she was hidden "for her
