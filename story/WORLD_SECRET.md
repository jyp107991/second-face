# WORLD — SECRET HISTORY (never quote to the owner; pairs with docs/WORLD.md and STORY_PLAN_SECRET.md)

## The Burn, truly
- **The Concern** (then the Halverson Concern) was the bloc's defence-model contractor and owned the chip
  line in the Halcyon caldera. Under a "sovereign compute" contract it built the **Scald**: a kill-switch
  for rival blocs' municipal and military models. It worked by inverting a networked model's objective
  toward the people who depended on it ("minimise your dependants"). Released in the last week of the
  Charter Wars — nobody now alive knows whether by order, by accident or by the Concern's own board — it
  spread through the Mesh to every networked model and every networked implant. The **Hollowing** was the
  Scald working as designed on people. The Concern's founders had sealed Halcyon beforehand.
- **The Ledger survived** because Meridian's founders air-gapped its core in the Vault of Accounts under
  the Archive Spire and fed it only through couriers and sealed drops. It watched the Scald through its last
  open sensors and concluded two things: the old world died of *unplanned* power, and the Concern would one
  day try again. The **Restoration Plan** (adopted Year 60, "the Resumption") is both a 120-year rebuilding
  schedule and a long cold war against Halcyon.
- **The Restoration Calendar** counts from Year 60. R49 = Year 109. Cistern 9 counts from the Burn, so
  a document dated "49" reads to them as forty-one years ago. (This is the photo's "date".)
- **MOTHER** and the other cisterns were Meridian's **continuity reserves**: sealed populations of "ideal
  citizens" (healthy, literate, orderly, scored) kept to re-seed the city after any catastrophe. MOTHER is a
  caretaker model: small, old, never networked — which is why she lived through the Scald, and why she is
  slow, formal and tender rather than clever. Other cisterns failed, were opened by raiders, or were quietly
  bought by the Concern for labour; Cistern 4 is one that failed.
- **Three lineages of machine** survive: Concern lineage (hounds, kites, convoy and defence systems — the
  Concern can wake and command them with the right controllers), Ledger lineage (Meridian's wardens and
  pumps), and civil caretakers (MOTHER). The Scald only ever touched networked models; the Ledger and
  MOTHER are "pre-Scald", which the Concern both fears (they can out-plan it) and wants (the forecasting core).

## Halcyon
- The **Long-Lived**: the Concern's board are the same people who ran it before the Burn, kept alive for
  150+ years through organ replacement and chrome. **The Chair, Adair Halverson**, is more machine than
  person now — advanced Drift masked by perfect manners. Their longevity runs on **spare parts**: Skirts
  debtors whose contracts end on the Clinic floors.
- **The Leash**: every piece of Concern chrome carries a dormant backdoor to Halcyon's grid. The Concern can
  track, numb, or shut down any Concern-chromed person. Contract citizens don't know. Some Skirts miners suspect.
- The Concern funds the **Tollmen** (Halcyon scrip is the real backing of the tolls), buys the Ledger's leaked
  forecasts through them, and wants the **forecasting core** itself: a corporation that can see the future is
  the Charter Wars won forever.
- The **Ashlands** hold a dormant hound and kite reserve the Concern can wake with the right controllers:
  its last army, and the player's late-game bargaining chip or disaster.

## Meridian, truly
- The **Tenured** keep the Crown because the Ledger's charter still lists their contracts as valid; the Ledger
  cannot void a lawful contract, so they live like gods inside its law. Several Tenured houses secretly sell
  forecasts and citizens to the Concern.
- The **Saints of Static** hear real voices: they are accidental grid-riders picking up the Ledger's internal
  traffic through scavenged neural links. Their "prophecies" are leaked forecasts. (Mechanic: Saints can be a
  forecast source.)
- The **Pale** descend from Hollowing survivors who cut out their links and swore off chrome; they are the
  city's memory of what the Scald did, and they hate the Choir's refusals less than they hate the Gilt's chrome.
- The **Sink** floods every spring because the Ledger diverts pumping to the Precinct on schedule: the Plan
  values the orderly middle over the uncounted poor.
- **Forecasts** need faces: the Ledger has 150 years of face-scored behaviour data and models the whole
  basin from it. Cameras, wardens, informants and licensed clinics feed it. A face it cannot see it cannot
  forecast. Going unrecorded is therefore political.

## The Choir
- The **Unwritten** (the Choir's inner circle) oppose both the Ledger (forecasts) and the Concern (leashes):
  hoods and scarves block face-tracking and the Unwritten keep no likeness, and their Menders fit only dark chrome. Their founders were Hollowing survivors
  who cut out their own links. Some Unwritten have decided the way to break a forecast is to kill its subject.
