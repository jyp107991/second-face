# WORLD — SECRET HISTORY (never quote to the owner; pairs with docs/WORLD.md and STORY_PLAN_SECRET §7–§8)

## The Burn, truly
- The Charter Wars were won and lost by machines. The **Halverson Concern** (today "the Concern") built the
  **Scald**: a data-weapon meant to kill rival municipal intelligences and the networked populations that fed them.
  It escaped its targets and burned the whole Mesh. The **Hollowing** (networked minds going quiet) was the
  Scald working as designed on people. The Concern's founders had sealed themselves in Halcyon beforehand.
- **The Ledger survived** because Meridian's founders air-gapped its core in the Vault of Accounts under the
  Archive Spire. It watched the Scald happen through its last open sensors and concluded two things: the old
  world died of *unplanned* power, and the Concern would one day try again. The Restoration Plan is both a
  rebuilding schedule and a long cold war against Halcyon.
- **MOTHER** and the other cisterns were Meridian reserve populations (see §7.1 of the plan). Other cisterns
  failed, were opened by raiders, or were quietly bought by the Concern for labour; Cistern 4 is one that failed.

## Halcyon
- The **Long-Lived**: the Concern's board are the same people who ran it before the Burn, kept alive for 120+
  years through organ replacement and chrome. **The Chair, Adair Halverson**, is more machine than person now —
  advanced Drift masked by perfect manners. Their longevity runs on **spare parts**: Skirts debtors whose
  contracts end in the Clinic floors.
- **The Leash**: every piece of Concern chrome carries a dormant backdoor to Halcyon's grid. The Concern can track,
  numb, or shut down any Concern-chromed person. Contract citizens don't know. Some Skirts miners suspect.
- The Concern funds the **Tollmen** (Halcyon scrip is the real backing of the tolls), buys the Ledger's leaked
  forecasts through them, and wants the **forecasting core** itself: a corporation that can see the future is
  the Charter Wars won forever.

## Meridian, truly
- The **Tenured** keep the Crown because the Ledger's charter still lists their contracts as valid; the Ledger
  cannot void a lawful contract, so they live like gods inside its law. Several Tenured houses secretly sell
  forecasts and citizens to the Concern.
- The **Saints of Static** hear real voices: they are accidental grid-riders picking up the Ledger's internal
  traffic through scavenged neural links. Their "prophecies" are leaked forecasts. (Mechanic: Saints can be a
  forecast source.)
- The **Sink** floods every spring because the Ledger diverts pumping to the Precinct on schedule: the Plan
  values the orderly middle over the uncounted poor.

## The Choir
- The Unwritten oppose both the Ledger (forecasts) and the Concern (leashes): masks block face-tracking, and
  their Menders fit only dark chrome. Their founders were Hollowing survivors who cut out their own links.
