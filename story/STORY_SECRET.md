# SECRET PLOT – do not show the player

## Title: THE SECOND FACE

### Setting
Ninety years after the Burn. The Sallow wasteland. Cistern 9, a sealed shelter of ~60 people run by an old-world caretaker computer the residents call MOTHER.

### Hook (player knows this)
A dying stranger in a battered mask hammers on the sealed door — the first outsider in living memory. He dies before speaking. In his pack: a photograph of the player, older, standing in a ruined city the player has never seen, dated 41 years ago. The elders send the player out to learn who he was.

### Hidden truth (layered reveals)
1. **Cistern 9 is a cycle.** MOTHER runs a pre-war experiment: preserve "ideal citizens" by regrowing the same people from stored genetic templates and partial memory imprints. Every ~40 years the population is quietly "renewed" (euthanised during a fake sickness and regrown as children). Residents never notice because records are rewritten.
2. **The stranger was the player's previous iteration's companion; the photo shows the previous player**, who escaped the last renewal 41 years ago.
3. **The previous player is alive** — now the old, revered leader ("the Founder") of the Choir of the Open Sky, the faction that seems most benevolent. They have spent decades planning to destroy Cistern 9 and every other cycling shelter, believing them abominations. They sent the stranger to fetch the player — a "fresh" self they want as a successor, or as the only one who can open MOTHER's core (genetic lock).
4. **Late twist:** MOTHER's templates are the only surviving pre-war seed bank of human/plant/animal genetic diversity in the region. Destroying the shelter dooms long-term recovery; keeping it means the cycle of killing continues.

### Factions
- **Shale** (town) — frontier town, sheriff Ada Vey, pressured by the Rust Dogs raiders.
- **Rust Dogs** — raiders; their leader Kessler bought the stranger's map and knows where he came from.
- **The Tollmen** — highway merchant guild; want MOTHER's tech to clone labour.
- **Choir of the Open Sky** — appear kindly (healers, farmers); led secretly by the Founder (older player).
- **The Ledger** — pre-war city AI enforcing old laws; holds archived Cistern records proving the cycle.

### Acts
- **Act 1:** Leave Cistern 9 → Shale → Rust Dogs camp. Learn the stranger came from the east and carried Choir symbols. (First playable build ends here.)
- **Act 2:** Ledger archives (reveal 1–2), Tollmen bargain, Choir sanctuary. Founder hidden behind a mask; NPCs remark the player "looks familiar."
- **Act 3:** Meet the Founder (reveal 3). Return to Cistern 9 for confrontation with MOTHER (reveal 4). A renewal is scheduled imminently — the people you grew up with are about to die.

### Endings
- Side with the Founder: destroy MOTHER and the seed bank. People freed, future bleaker.
- Side with MOTHER: cycle continues, seed bank preserved; player may be "renewed."
- Sell to Tollmen: cloned labour empire.
- Hidden: reprogram MOTHER to stop renewals and release the seed bank to the wasteland (needs high Science/Speech, Ledger records, and the Founder's trust; the Founder sacrifices themself to unlock the core).
- Epilogue slides for each town/faction.
